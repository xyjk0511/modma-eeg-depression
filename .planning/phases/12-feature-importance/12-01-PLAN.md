_loso_collect_coefs(X, y, n_components=20, C=1.0) -> np.ndarray (n_folds, 128):
- LeaveOneOut LOSO; fit on training indices only per fold
- Per fold: StandardScaler -> PCA(n_components) -> LogisticRegression(C=C, max_iter=2000, class_weight="balanced")
- Back-project: pca.components_.T @ clf.coef_[0]  (coef_ is (1,20) for binary LR; use [0])
- Return np.array(fold_weights) shape (n_folds, 128)
